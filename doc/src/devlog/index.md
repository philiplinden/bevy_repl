# Devlog

These are my working notes from building BevyREPL, kept in the order I wrote
them. They capture what I was thinking at the time, including ideas that were
later dropped, so they don't always match how the crate works today. For the
current design, start at
[Vision for BevyREPL](../design/index.md).

The log falls roughly into three stretches:

- [**The ratatui Prototype**](ratatui-prototype/index.md) (August 2025,
  v0.1–v0.4)
- [**Keeping Up with Bevy**](keeping-up-with-bevy/index.md) (October 2025 –
  March 2026, v0.5)
- [**The Crossterm Rewrite**](crossterm-rewrite/index.md) (August 2026 onward,
  toward v1.0)
